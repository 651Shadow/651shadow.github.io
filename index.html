About Me

My full name is Abdelrahman Magdy Hamida (which is why my primary email includes my middle name, abdelrahman.m.magdy@gmail.com, while my engineering handle on GitHub is @651Shadow). I am pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science Technology (Network & Cyber Security) at Elsewedy University of Technology (SUTech), graduating in 2029.

Most impactful bugs I remediate aren't exotic zero-days. They are missing Postgres RLS column guards, O(N) password-hash loops exposed on API lookup paths, credit balances updated without non-negative DB constraints, or SSRF proxies lacking post-DNS IP filtering.

Everything presented here is verifiable in production deployments, source repositories, Cisco Packet Tracer topologies, and sanitized audit PDFs you can inspect on this page. Alongside university coursework and building production systems, I am actively preparing for CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) and Cisco CCST Cybersecurity / CCNA (no commercial certificates held yet).
