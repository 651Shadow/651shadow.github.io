About Me

I'm a B.Tech student in Computer Science Technology (Network & Cyber Security) at Elsewedy University of Technology, graduating in 2029. Outside classes, I build full-stack web tools and audit early-stage SaaS apps.

Most real bugs I come across aren't exotic zero-days. They're missing Supabase RLS rules, credit balances checked before an upstream call instead of locked atomically, or proxy endpoints that follow redirects into localhost.

I don't hold commercial certificates yet, so everything I claim is backed by live deployments, Packet Tracer topologies, and sanitized audit reports you can inspect right here.
